Support

Help for the website and The Dailies apps for iPhone and Mac.

How do I add a score?

Finish a daily game, tap its Share or Copy button, then tap Paste in The Dailies. In the app, games open inside the app with a Paste button at the bottom. On iPhone you can also pick The Dailies straight from a game's share sheet.

It says my result isn't from today

Only today's puzzle counts, and days roll over at midnight US Eastern time. Copy the result again from the game's own Share button rather than an old message.

How do friends work?

Your leaderboard is you plus the friends you've accepted. Add people by username on the Friends tab, or send them your invite link.

Someone is bothering me

Remove them on the Friends tab: they'll no longer see your scores or appear on your boards. To report a user or a suggestion, email us with their username and we'll look into it within 24 hours.

Deleting your account

Go to the You tab and tap Delete account. See the Privacy Policy for what that removes.
