Privacy Policy

Last updated October 27, 2026. Covers the website at thedailies.lol and The Dailies apps for iPhone and Mac.

The short version

The Dailies keeps only what it needs to run a leaderboard for you and your friends. There are no ads, no tracking, no analytics services, and we never sell or share your data.

What we store

  • Your account: your username and a securely hashed password (we can't see your password).
  • Your scores: the result text you paste or share from a game, the score we read from it, and when you added it.
  • Your friends: who you've friended and pending requests.
  • Your home page layout: which games you favorited and the order you put them in.
  • Game suggestions you send, and your votes on them.
  • Signed-in devices: if you use the app, a device name (like "iPhone") so the app can save scores for you. Only a scrambled version of each device's key is stored.

Who can see it

Your username is visible to anyone who searches for it to send a friend request. Your scores, results, stats and suggestions are visible only to you and the friends you've accepted.

Cookies and the in-app game browser

We use one cookie to keep you signed in. When you play a game inside the app, that game's own website runs as it would in a browser and may set its own cookies (for example, if you log in to it). Those stay on your device; we don't read them, and the game sites' own privacy policies apply to them. We don't add any code to game sites.

Where it lives

Your data is stored in a database hosted by Railway (railway.com) in the United States, and sent only over encrypted connections (HTTPS).

Deleting your data

You can delete your account at any time from the You tab (website or app): tap Delete account and confirm with your password. This permanently removes your account, scores, friendships, suggestions, votes and signed-in devices right away.

Children

The Dailies isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect data from them.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above. Questions or requests: support@thedailies.lol.
