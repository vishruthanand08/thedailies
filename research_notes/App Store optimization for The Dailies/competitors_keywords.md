# Competitive App Store landscape and keyword demand for a daily-puzzle friends leaderboard app (researched 2026-10-29, US storefront)

Note on dating: App Store pages were fetched on 2026-10-29. Apple pages often show version dates without a year; that is noted where it applies. "Measured" means it was read from a listing or tool page. "Estimate" or "Inference" means it was not.

## Which apps exist in this niche, and who uses the name "The Dailies"?

### Takeaway
The niche of tracking pasted scores from many games with friends is thinly served on the App Store. Every direct competitor found has fewer than 10 ratings: StreakSync, Daily Game League, Wurdle with Friends and Scordle. The real competition is NYT Games, which added a friends leaderboard in April 2025 but covers only NYT games, plus Discord, SMS and WhatsApp bots. No US App Store listing titled exactly "The Dailies" was found. The nearby names are habit trackers called "Dailies" and a puzzle app called "The Daily: Puzzles & Games".

### Inferences
- How competitors name and position themselves: they use "Tracker" or "Track" plus a game name in the subtitle ("Track Wordle & Daily Puzzles", "The score tracker for Wordle", "Puzzle Tracker"), plus a social hook ("Beat Friends", "with Friends", "League"). Categories vary: Utilities (StreakSync), Entertainment (Daily Game League) and Word (Scordle, DLEs). Nobody owns "Feed", "Crown" or a Strava-style framing.
- No direct competitor has meaningful scale (all under 10 ratings), so nobody dominates the long-tail tracker terms. StreakSync is the closest analogue (share extension, LinkedIn games, Mac). It is open source and actively updated, so it is the one to watch.
- NYT's built-in leaderboard leaves the cross-publisher gap open: NYT + LinkedIn + geography + sports. That is the defensible positioning ("all your daily games, one leaderboard").
- Name variants: "Dailies" alone collides with habit trackers, and "The Daily" is taken by a puzzle app. A variant naming the function, such as "The Dailies: Puzzle Leaderboard" or "Dailies - Friends Leaderboard", would be distinct from the habit trackers. The exact title should be checked in App Store Connect, since the "The Dailies" title collision the user reports was not visible in web search.

### Gaps
- I could not identify the specific App Store app that holds the name "The Dailies". Web search found no exact-title listing, so it may be reserved in App Store Connect without being live, or live outside the US.

- Keywords competitors target in their hidden 100-character field are not public. The AppGoblin keyword page for Puzzmo returned 401.

## Keyword demand: popularity and difficulty (US App Store)

### Takeaway
Public keyword data turned up only one measured data point: Applyra rates "wordle" at 72/100 traffic (high) and 62/100 difficulty, with the top 3 held by Wordle!, NYT Games and Word Guess. For the other target terms, no free source published popularity or difficulty, so only qualitative judgments are possible. Generic terms (puzzle, word game, brain games, trivia, crossword, daily puzzle) are dominated by large game publishers. Tracker and leaderboard long-tails have little competition but probably little volume.

### Cited Findings
- **"wordle" (Applyra, US iOS; collection date not stated, page © 2026, measured third-party model):** traffic 72/100 (High), difficulty 62/100 (Medium), KEI 1.16, competitor strength 92/100, top-3 strength 100/100, about 81k average installs for top apps. Top ranks: 1 Wordle!, 2 NYT Games, 3 Word Guess, 4 Wordscapes, 5 WORDLE - Word Puzzle Game, 6 Connect Words, 7 Words With Friends, 8 Wordaily, 9 Hangman, 10 Everyday Puzzles, 11 Cheatle (Wordle solver), 12 Wordus. The most common title words among top apps are word, game, puzzle, words, crossword and daily. — [Applyra: wordle](https://www.applyra.io/tools/keywords/ios/us/wordle)
- Applyra's free tool only serves pre-generated keyword pages. Hyphenated URLs for daily-puzzle, daily-games, word-game, leaderboard, streak, puzzle, trivia, crossword, connections, queens, strands, nyt-games, linkedin-games and others all returned 404 (tested 2026-10-29). — [Applyra keywords index](https://www.applyra.io/tools/keywords)

## Growth tactics that matter for ranking

### Takeaway
Every tool in this space grows through the group: invite links, group-text bots, Discord servers, WhatsApp groups and shareable scorecards. App Store ranking follows install velocity and ratings, so invite-driven installs plus prompting for ratings matter more than keyword stuffing, given how few ratings competitors have.

### Cited Findings
- NYT's leaderboard requires friends to install the NYT Games app and add each other, using friend-invite links. — [TODAY](https://www.today.com/popculture/nyt-multi-game-leaderboard-rcna202155)
- Discord bots need zero signup: players just paste in the channel, tied to their Discord identity. The free tier covers 5 players and paid tiers cover more. — [wordlediscordbot.com](https://www.wordlediscordbot.com/)
- An SMS bot joins an existing group text, Trackdle is added to WhatsApp groups, and Wordle Group uses email. — [kevin.clyne.com](https://kevin.clyne.com/wordles-with-friends); [Trackdle](https://www.producthunt.com/products/trackdle); [wordlegroup.com](https://wordlegroup.com/)
- Daily Game League offers shareable scorecards and weekly group competitions. — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/daily-game-league/id6550890849)
- StreakSync uses a share extension (log straight from the game's Share sheet), widgets and per-game reminders. — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/streaksync-puzzle-tracker/id6755203446)
- Puzzmo uses clubs (group leaderboards available to free users) and newspaper syndication. — [Puzzmo blog](https://blog-puzzmo-com-staging.vercel.app/posts/2024/07/24/groups-to-clubs); [INMA](https://www.inma.org/blogs/ideas/post.cfm/hearst-cracks-the-engagement-code-with-puzzmo)

### Inferences
- The Dailies already has the main levers: invite links with Open Graph previews, a share extension, push reminders, kudos and a feed. A shareable daily scorecard image or an iMessage-friendly recap would match the most common competitor tactic.
- Since rivals hold 1–6 ratings, even about 20–50 ratings would likely put The Dailies above all of them for tracker long-tails (estimate).

### Gaps
- I found no evidence of an iMessage app extension or GroupMe bot dedicated to multi-game tracking, and no Reddit (r/wordle, r/NYTConnections) thread data was retrieved on which tools groups actually use.
